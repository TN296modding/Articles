Iran, Ukraine, Campism & Anti-Imperialism

15/06/2026

If one is at all interacting with the online leftist space, especially in Marxist-Leninist or Marxist-Leninist-Maoist spaces, one will notice a big debate going on, which even bigger in 2022 and 2023 - The 2022 Invasion of Ukraine. Indeed, it appears, many contain nolstalgia for the Soviet Union (Despite many not being around, or having lived in the Soviet Union). They believe the old Communist system still lives within the Russian governemnt, when indeed, quite the opposite is true. They see the Invasion as a war between two capitalist countries - the Ukrainian State, as a proxy for the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation, and Russia. So, as that is true, they support Russia - but why? Well, due to a mix of Soviet nolstalgia, Anti-Westernism, and the belief that when Russia reclaims it's old lands, the workers of Russia will rise up and take power, reforming the Supreme Soviet. This of course, is a completely mistaken belief. The first thing that would stop this from becoming reality, is that the Russian workers have no appetite for Communism, or really, any sort of leftism. The second, is that while yes, Ukraine is very much a proxy state of NATO, Russia is just as, if not more imperialist than, NATO. Either country you may support is directly supporting imperialism, so the question now becomes: "Do you support the right of nations to exist?". In a vacuum, the Marxist and Anarchist views should be "No, the state is a tool of the bourgeoisie to oppress the workers" - However, in the context of the current state of the world, a Capitalist world, then indeed the State (Whether Capitalist or Communist/Leftist) should exist, as if a State were to not exist, another State would claim the unclaimed land, which could be bad for the citizens of the previously unclaimed land. One of the few things a Capitalist state is good for, is protecting the major ethnicity of the land against foriegn oppression - It has a vested interest in this. So, if Ukraine is state, of the Ukrainian people, existing partially to protect the Ukrainian people against foreign oppression, than it does indeed have the right to exist, as Russia is a foreign state known for oppressing non "true" Russian ethnicities, like the Buryats. Now to compare the to Iran - Campists will support Iran under the guise of anti-imperialism, yet the true reason the support Iran is because it is anti-western - After all, if they were anti-imperialist, they would support Ukraine, as Russia, trying to gain more land, and known for its oppression of minorities it considered non-Russian, is an imperialist state.
