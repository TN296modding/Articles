Iran, Ukraine, Campism & Anti-Imperialism

15/06/2026

If one is at all interacting with the online leftist space, especially in Marxist-Leninist or Marxist-Leninist-Maoist spaces, one will notice a big debate going on, which even bigger in 2022 and 2023 - The 2022 Invasion of Ukraine. Indeed, it appears, many contain nolstalgia for the Soviet Union (Despite many not being around, or having lived in the Soviet Union). They believe the old Communist system still lives within the Russian governemnt, when indeed, quite the opposite is true. They see the Invasion as a war between two capitalist countries - the Ukrainian State, as a proxy for the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation, and Russia. So, as that is true, they support Russia - but why? Well, due to a mix of Soviet nolstalgia, Anti-Westernism, and the belief that when Russia reclaims it's old lands, the workers of Russia will rise up and take power, reforming the Supreme Soviet.
